## Blocking limitations

- **Held-out data isolation:** the five local 1h Futures files are mixed-year sources. Current preparation may read full Feather files before filtering. No prebuilt bounded 2022–2024 panel is retained in the research panel directory, and this session did not demonstrate a research process that cannot read 2025+ data.
- **Agent security boundary:** OpenCode policy text and gateway tests are application controls. The installed runtime could not be inspected here because its log directory is read-only. Network denial, effective shell policy, symlink/ancestor defenses under hostile filesystem races, and OS-level file isolation are not established. Arbitrary Python candidate execution remains disabled.
- **Inference calibration:** the cross-sectional MBB and tested HAC configurations failed the prespecified calibration requirements. They are diagnostic only. No inferential method currently has `PASS` status and formal cross-sectional `SUPPORTED` claims remain closed. Descriptive/adjustment `PASS` entries do not authorize inference.
- **Current-HEAD real replay:** the prior five-asset report predates HEAD and its bounded input panel is not present. A same-HEAD complete Evidence/Decision/replay comparison was therefore not performed.
- **Legacy store schema:** the local SQLite store has integrity but contains no v1.1 cross-sectional evidence/decision tables. Synthetic tests verify behavior on temporary stores; they do not prove migration or append-only enforcement on a production v1.1 store.
- **Remote governance:** local CODEOWNERS and CI configuration are present, but required CODEOWNER review and branch protection are remote settings and were not independently verified.

## Interpretation limits that remain in force

- 2022–2024 is reused development data, not fresh out-of-sample data.
- Prior reports and old evidence retain their original engine, protocol, code, and method context. Older run manifests without version fields cannot be presumed compatible with the current engine.
- Cross-sectional targets based on next-open/forward-close prices are research proxies; they are not executable fills or trading PnL.

- Fundamental data snapshots without verified point-in-time evidence remain unavailable to strict research mode.
